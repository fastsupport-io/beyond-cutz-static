# Chosen Direction — Precision Landscape Studio

## Design Movement

**New American landscape modernism**, combining restrained editorial typography with the practical drafting language of residential and commercial grounds planning.

## Core Principles

1. **Cut with intent:** Diagonal mowing-stripe compositions and controlled horizontal rules make every surface feel measured, not decorated.
2. **Local proof over empty claims:** Hyperlocal service-area language, clear service scenarios, and practical answers establish credibility for Midlothian and Chesterfield customers.
3. **Purposeful conversion:** Each section has one job—qualify, reassure, or move a homeowner/property manager toward a request.
4. **Durable clarity:** High contrast, large type, and structured information make the site legible on a driveway, on mobile, and to search engines and AI answer systems.

## Color Philosophy

The base is **warm limestone and field-paper cream**, giving the business the calm assurance of a well-kept property rather than a loud promotional ad. **Mowline Green** is the ownable signal color: a saturated green with a little yellow energy, reserved for decisive actions and precision marks. Ink-black green grounds the interface and supports a premium commercial audience. The green is not a background wash; it is a deliberate proof of care.

## Layout Paradigm

The page uses a **field-plan narrative** rather than a centered-card grid: a narrow left index rail, asymmetric content blocks that advance like property zones, and large imagery broken by drafting annotations. On smaller screens, the rail becomes a compact project index, preserving the story sequence without losing hierarchy.

## Signature Elements

1. **Mowline stripes:** Fine offset lines, set at an 18-degree field angle, appear as section dividers and hover details.
2. **Site markers:** Small coordinate-like labels such as “01 / WEEKLY CARE” reinforce the planning motif.
3. **Cutout rule:** Angled green blocks appear at the edge of primary CTAs and content zones, suggesting a clean mower pass.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should communicate responsiveness and order: primary buttons shift a short mowline forward, navigation indicators slide to the selected section, and calculators/forms explain the next action without surprising the visitor. Click and keyboard actions remain instant and clear.

## Animation

Use brief 160–260ms transform-and-opacity transitions only. Section entrances reveal from a 12px vertical offset in staggered sequence; mower-line rules draw laterally once per section. No continuous decorative motion. Honor reduced-motion preferences by showing final states immediately.

## Typography System

**DM Serif Display** provides grounded, confident display headlines with occasional italic emphasis. **Manrope** supplies the compact, clear operational voice for body copy, labels, and UI. Headlines use tight tracking and a small amount of deliberate asymmetry; labels are uppercase, letter-spaced, and never oversized. No Inter.

## Brand Essence

**Beyond Cutz is the precise, local lawn-care partner for Central Virginia homeowners and property managers who want their property handled reliably—not generically.**  
**Personality:** Methodical, neighborly, assured.

## Brand Voice

Speak in direct, local, practical language. Headlines name the outcome; CTAs name the action. Avoid inflated promises and generic welcome copy.

> “Your lawn’s weekly reset—without another thing on your list.”

> “Tell us about the property. We’ll map the next cut.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The logo is a **three-pass mowline mark**: three rounded, ascending lawn stripes cut through a tilted field rectangle. It is used as a bold green symbol beside a custom serif/sans wordmark, with the stand-alone mark serving as favicon and mobile navigation identifier.

## Signature Brand Color

**Mowline Green — #75B82A**

## Implementation Notes

The live operational CTAs will be designed to accept a GoHighLevel booking-calendar URL, phone number, webchat widget, and payment/estimate workflow once supplied. The WordPress deployment should use editable sections and custom post types for services, service areas, FAQs, and seasonal advice.

## Style Decisions

- **Palette rule:** Mowline Green `#75B82A` is reserved for CTAs, arrows, numerals, thin rules, and precision marks; large background fields remain limestone, field-paper cream, or ink-black green unless a seasonal editorial accent is deliberately approved.
- **Public copy rule:** Customer-facing pages never expose launch status, platform names, embeds, or implementation instructions. Unavailable operational detail is translated into a calm, service-oriented next step.
- **Service-page rule:** Residential pages visibly center homeowner relief and a cared-for return home; commercial pages visibly center accountable site management, property standards, and clear communication.
